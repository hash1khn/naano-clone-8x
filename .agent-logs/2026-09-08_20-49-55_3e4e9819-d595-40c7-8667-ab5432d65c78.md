This was a real auth bug, not just you picking the wrong screen.

**What went wrong**
1. Google signup only set `users.role = creator` — it never created a `creator_profiles` row, so there was nothing to show in marketplace/deals.
2. Logging in with Google from `/login` (no role cookie) defaulted **new** accounts to **brand**.
3. Google always shows the account picker. A different Google account is a different user — the original creator stays on the first account.

**What I fixed**
- Creator signup/login now creates (or backfills) a `creator_profiles` row.
- First-time Google login without a role no longer silently becomes brand — you’re asked to choose creator vs brand.
- Email register/login also provisions the creator profile.

**What you should do**
1. Sign out.
2. Sign in with Google and pick the **same** Google account you used when you signed up as creator.
3. If you’re sent to “Finish signing up”, choose **I’m a creator**.

If you already logged in with a different Google account and it was saved as brand, that second account won’t show the first profile — use the original Google account, or tell me the emails and I can help straighten the DB roles.
