model: grok-4.6

Yes. After Google login, the app keeps you signed in with **HTTP cookies**, the same way email login does.

When Google sends you back to `/api/auth/callback`, the app exchanges the OAuth `code` for a Supabase session and writes that session onto the response. Those cookies cover the whole site (`path=/`, `SameSite=Lax`, `Secure` on HTTPS).

On later requests, `proxy.ts` reads those cookies and refreshes the session if the access token is close to expiring, so you stay logged in across page loads and tabs in the same browser.

It also stores (or reuses) your row in `public.users`, including `role`. If you already have a brand or creator account, that role is kept. New Google users default to **brand** unless a temporary `oauth_role` cookie was set.

What is **not** kept after login: the short-lived `oauth_role` cookie (it is cleared on callback). Closing the browser does not log you out as long as the cookies remain; you stay signed in until the refresh token expires or you sign out.
